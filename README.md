# Airline Passenger Satisfaction & Service Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes airline passenger satisfaction and service performance using Power BI. The objective is to identify factors contributing to passenger dissatisfaction and highlight service areas that require improvement.

## Business Problem

Although approximately 82% of passengers are returning customers, overall passenger satisfaction is only about 43%. This analysis investigates the factors associated with passenger satisfaction and identifies service areas where improvements can enhance customer experience and loyalty.

## Dataset

- 130K+ passenger records
- 24 fields
- Demographic information: Age, Gender, Customer Type
- Travel information: Class, Type of Travel, Distance
- Operational metrics: Arrival and Departure Delays
- 14 service-rating attributes
- Passenger Satisfaction: Satisfied / Neutral or Dissatisfied

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX

## Analysis Performed

- Analyzed overall passenger satisfaction
- Compared first-time and returning passengers
- Analyzed satisfaction across travel classes and customer segments
- Examined departure and arrival delays
- Created age-group segmentation
- Analyzed ratings across 14 airline service areas
- Compared service ratings between first-time and returning passengers
- Built interactive filters for deeper segment-level analysis

## Dashboard

The dashboard consists of two main analytical views:

### Page 1 — Passenger Satisfaction Overview

Includes:
- Total customers
- Satisfaction percentage
- Customer loyalty
- Gender distribution
- Satisfaction breakdown
- Arrival and departure delay analysis
- Age-group analysis
- Customer-type analysis
- Interactive filters

### Page 2 — Service Analysis

Includes:
- Average service ratings
- Service comparison by customer type
- Highest- and lowest-rated services
- Average arrival and departure delays
- Interactive filters for customer type, class, travel type and satisfaction

## Key Insights

- 43.45% of passengers were satisfied.
- 81.69% of passengers were returning customers.
- Average departure delay was 14.71 minutes.
- Average arrival delay was 15.10 minutes.
- In-flight service received the highest average rating at 3.64/5.
- Baggage handling received an average rating of 3.63/5.
- In-flight Wi-Fi received a relatively low average rating of 2.73/5.
- Ease of online booking received a relatively low average rating of 2.76/5.
- Returning customers rated most services higher than first-time passengers.

## Recommendations

- Improve digital touchpoints such as online booking and airport gate experience.
- Improve in-flight Wi-Fi services.
- Provide a smoother check-in and onboarding experience for first-time passengers.
- Leverage strong service areas such as baggage handling and in-flight service in customer-facing initiatives.

## Business Impact

The dashboard provides a data-driven view of passenger satisfaction and service performance, helping identify areas for service improvement, customer retention and loyalty enhancement.
